Kurvenrausch

Classic pseudo-3D bitmap racer

Play in the browser

A 320×240 picture (640×480 in HD), scaled up with chunky pixels and drawn on the GPU with WebGL, or in software (F8 switches). Built to WebAssembly with Emscripten and packaged with Nix.

About

In the spirit of OutRun, Lotus Esprit Turbo Challenge and Pole Position: one lap around the world with changing weather, cliff roads, forks in the road, traffic, police chases, a synthesised engine and a radio, from sunrise to a night lit by street lamps, neon and headlights. Written in C++17 with SDL2, with two renderers that draw the same picture: a classic software renderer and an OpenGL ES 2 one. All art is generated procedurally as pixel art at startup; all sound is synthesised on the fly — there are no asset files.

Sixteen zones

France, England, the Netherlands, Germany, Switzerland, Italy, Egypt, Kenya, India, Korea, Japan, Australia, Arizona, California, San Francisco, Brazil — each with its own scenery, weather and road markings.

Controls

  • ↑ / W or right trigger — accelerate
  • ↓ / S or left trigger — brake / reverse
  • ← → / A D or left stick — steer
  • Space / Y — nitro
  • Ctrl or left shoulder — handbrake
  • H / X — horn
  • C / Back — camera view
  • N or right stick — radio
  • L or D-pad up — headlights
  • F8 — switch renderer (GPU / software)
  • P / Esc / Start — pause menu

On a touch screen, controls appear once you touch it: drag on the left to steer, the pedals and buttons are on the right.
